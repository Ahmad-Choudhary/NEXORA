1. Data We Collect

To provide our screening and guidance features, NEXORA collects the following information:

  • Account information your full name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • PHQ-9 and lifestyle screening responses your answers to the assessment questions, the resulting score, severity classification, and lifestyle risk indicators.
  • Usage data pages visited and basic interaction data used to keep the platform working correctly.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your screening responses are used solely to generate your personalized PHQ-9 result and lifestyle risk indicator. To do this:

  • Your assessment answers are sent to our machine learning model, which is currently hosted on a separate research environment (Google Colab, accessed via a secure tunnel) for processing.
  • The resulting score, severity, and risk level are sent back to our servers and stored against your account so you can view your history.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties, and we do not use your screening responses for advertising.

3. Confidentiality

Your assessment history is only visible to you when logged into your own account. We treat your mental health information as sensitive data and limit internal access to what is strictly necessary to operate and maintain the platform.

4. Security Measures

  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard hashing (bcrypt) before being stored we cannot see your actual password, even internally.
  • Your data is stored in a MySQL database accessible only through authenticated backend services.
  • We recommend using a unique password for your NEXORA account and not sharing your login credentials with anyone.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the assessment history stored under your account at any time via the "View Past Results" section.
  • Request correction of inaccurate account information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

6. Children's Privacy

NEXORA is intended for general audiences seeking mental health screening and guidance. If you are a minor, please use this platform with the awareness and support of a parent, guardian, or trusted adult, and always involve a qualified professional for anything beyond general self-help information.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the platform evolves. Continued use of NEXORA after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
